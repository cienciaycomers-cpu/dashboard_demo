# FM Signal - Product strategy

## Problem

The current dashboard proves the analytical model but presents it as a conventional reporting interface. The portfolio needs a second experience that makes the user's paid-media reasoning and product-design ability immediately legible.

## Target visitor

Recruiters, prospective clients and collaborators reviewing the portfolio on desktop or mobile without prior onboarding.

## Scope

### Included

- Landing selector for `Classic Dashboard` and `FM Signal`.
- New `/signal` experience with vertical narrative scroll.
- Existing filters, metrics, comparison logic and alert behavior.
- Profitability-led opening scene.
- Acquisition, conversion and profitability chapters.
- Hypothesis-based close with evidence for scaling, maintaining or watching investment.
- Responsive and reduced-motion behavior.

### Excluded

- Live ad-platform connectors.
- New data sources or changes to the demo dataset.
- Replacing the current `/` experience before explicit review.
- Automatic budget changes or prescriptive automation.

## User stories

- As a portfolio visitor, I want to understand the business decision behind the metrics quickly.
- As a paid-media reviewer, I want to inspect ACOS, margin and break-even context without losing detail.
- As a potential client, I want to see an opportunity hypothesis and the evidence behind it.
- As a mobile visitor, I want the narrative to remain usable and legible.

## Success criteria

- First-minute comprehension of profitability and investment opportunity.
- Clear distinction between fact, alert and hypothesis.
- No loss of current dashboard capabilities.
- Memorable visual identity for FM Signal.
